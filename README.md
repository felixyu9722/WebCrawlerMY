## 🌟 Key Features / 核心特性

### 🇬🇧 English
* **🕷️ Cyber-Mechanical Spider (IK Walking Gait)**: 8-legged inverse kinematics procedural animation with natural biomechanical locomotion, respiratory breathing rhythm, and autonomous exploration.
* **🌐 Holographic Star Map Radar**: Floating 360° radar sweep HUD anchored at the viewport corner with real-time target scanning.
* **🎵 Generative Ambient Audio ("Arachnid Pulse")**: Built-in procedural Web Audio synthesizer with smooth fade-in and interactive volume control.
* **🕸️ Luminous Web Energy Cache**: Collects prey & text capsules back to the side energy web, dynamically weaving glowing spider webs.
* **🪰 Digital Firefly Ecosystem & Prey Mode**: Auto-spawning digital prey with evasion AI; triggers spider red-eye hunt mode.
* **🎭 Cinematic Acrobatics**: Dynamic jumpscare jumps and ceiling silk rappelling drops.

### 🇨🇳 中文
* **🕷️ 拟物化机械智能大蜘蛛**：8 肢精准反向运动学（IK）行走步态、呼吸律动、触角探测与自主漫游。
* **🌐 全息星图雷达 (Radar)**：固定悬浮于屏幕右下角，随视口流畅游走，支持 360° 连续波束扫描与星图调度。
* **🎵 Arachnid Pulse 原创氛围背景音乐**：左下角音量与播放控制器，开局默认 16% 音量，智能无缝唤醒。
* **🕸️ 侧边发光蛛网储食基地**：累积抓取文字药丸归巢挂存，织造动态发光能量蛛网。
* **🪰 数码飞虫生态与红眼暴走追捕**：每 30 秒或手动投喂，飞虫智能远距避让与逃窜，蜘蛛红眼锁定追猎。
* **🎭 机械巨蛛特技演出**：扑面突脸与天顶吐丝倒挂吊落。
